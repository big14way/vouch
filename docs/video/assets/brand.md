# Kora Coffee — brand guide

## Palette
- Roast Brown `#4A2C1D` — primary: logo, headings, packaging
- Crema `#F3E6D3` — backgrounds and the highlight in the mark
- Ember `#C8773B` — accents only: prices, calls to action, the "COFFEE" line

## Typefaces
- Headings: Fraunces Bold, generous letter spacing on the wordmark
- Body: Inter Regular, 16 px minimum on screen

## Usage rules
1. Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the bean mark.
2. Never place the logo on Ember; use Roast Brown on Crema, or Crema on Roast Brown.
3. Do not stretch, rotate or recolour the mark; scale it proportionally only.
