# Scope: landing page copy

Deliver `copy.md` (Markdown) with the copy for a landing page, and `alt.md` with two alternative headlines.

`copy.md` must contain, in this order:
1. A headline of at most 8 words.
2. A subheadline of at most 25 words.
3. Three benefit blocks, each a `###` title plus one paragraph of at most 40 words.
4. One call-to-action button label of at most 4 words.

Tone: plain and specific. No exclamation marks anywhere. Do not mention competitors.
