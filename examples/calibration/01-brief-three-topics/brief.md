# Freelancer payment risk: what the evidence says

## Late payment
- 71% of freelancers report being paid late at least once in the past year, and the median delay is 12 days past the agreed date [Freelancers Union survey, 2023].
- Late payment is concentrated in small clients: invoices under $1,000 are paid late twice as often as invoices over $5,000 [Bonsai invoicing data, 2024].
- Chasing invoices costs an average of 3.5 hours a month per freelancer, unpaid [Xero small business report, 2023].
Recommendation: hold the payment before work starts rather than adding another reminder tool after the invoice is issued.

## Scope disputes
- Half of non-payment cases begin as a disagreement about what was ordered, not a refusal to pay [IPSE self-employment report, 2023].
- Written scopes cut disputes sharply: freelancers who attach a written brief to every job report 60% fewer payment disputes [Bonsai invoicing data, 2024].
- Most disputes are small: the median disputed amount is under $400, too little for any formal process [Upwork dispute statistics, 2024].
Recommendation: make the written scope the object the payment is locked against, so the dispute has a reference document from day one.

## Cross-border fees
- International transfers cost freelancers 4% to 7% of the invoice once bank fees and currency spreads are counted [World Bank remittance prices, 2024].
- Settlement takes two to five business days on the correspondent banking route, and longer when a compliance check is triggered [SWIFT gpi observer report, 2023].
- Stablecoin settlement removes the spread but shifts the cost to the off-ramp, which averages 1% to 2% in most markets [Chainalysis geography of crypto report, 2024].
Recommendation: settle in stablecoins and let the worker choose the off-ramp, and show the all-in cost before the client locks funds.
