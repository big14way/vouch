# Scope: one-page brief on freelancer payment risk

Deliver a single Markdown file named `brief.md`, at most 500 words, for a client who is deciding whether to build a payments product for freelancers.

Requirements:
1. Cover exactly three topics, each as its own `##` section: late payment, scope disputes, cross-border fees.
2. Each section gives three findings as bullet points and ends with one line starting with `Recommendation:`.
3. Each finding that quotes a number names its source in brackets, for example `[Freelancers Union survey, 2023]`. No URLs needed.
4. Plain English, no marketing tone, no headings other than a title and the three sections.
