# Get paid when the work is verified

Lock the client's payment against a written scope. An independent check compares the delivery to the scope, and the money moves when it matches.

### Nothing to chase
The payment is locked before you start. When the delivery is verified, it settles to your wallet without an invoice, a reminder, or a phone call.

### One document decides
The scope you both agreed to is the reference for the whole job. If there is a disagreement, an arbiter reads the same scope and the same evidence.

### Works across borders
Settle in stablecoins on Tempo or Base and choose your own off-ramp. The all-in cost is shown before the client locks the funds.
